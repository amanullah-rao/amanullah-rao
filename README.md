# Hi, I'm Amanullah 👋

**GKS Scholar** and **M.S. student in AI Convergence** at
[Kunsan National University](https://www.kunsan.ac.kr), South Korea, in the
ICT Convergence Lab advised by Prof. Han-Gue Jo (brain and cognitive science,
artificial intelligence, medical information). I work on AI for physiological
computing, wearable sensing and medical applications, and I enjoy turning
research into working systems.

- Physiological computing and wearable AI

- Computer vision
- Human–robot and human–computer interaction
